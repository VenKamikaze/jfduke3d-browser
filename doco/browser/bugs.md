# Lesser bugs in the original game

Bugs found in upstream jfduke3d, jfbuild, jfaudiolib or vnet (at the pinned commits) while doing the browser work that **don't break the game**, or that break it only **in a feature a normal multiplayer game is unlikely to use** (marked **not planned**). Game-breaking, reproducible bugs get their own fix brief instead (see "Bugs found in the original game" in [`README.md`](README.md)). Nothing here has been sent upstream.

Each entry: where, what happens, how it was found, and its state (**open**, **worked around** in a brief, **fixed** on a `fix/` branch, or **not planned**).

**Numbering.** The engine forks (jfbuild, jfaudiolib, jfmact) and vnet are shared with Shadow Warrior, and so are their bugs: those keep jfSW's **L-numbers**, which live in [`jfsw-browser`'s `doco/browser/bugs.md`](https://github.com/VenKamikaze/jfsw-browser/blob/claude/doco/browser/bugs.md) (L1 to L34 at the time of writing; for example L9 stale `libengine.a`, L10 `syncstate`, L15 `ar rc`, L18 a lost relay session, L24/L33 refused pointer lock, L31/L34 vnet's WebTransport). Don't copy them here; add a line below only when a Duke brief meets one and has something to say about it. Bugs in **Duke's own code** get **D-numbers** (D1, D2, …) in the table below, so the two sets never collide. (This split is the implementer's choice in brief 00; the coordinator may change it.)

| # | Where | What happens | Found in | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L18 (rest) | `src/game.c`, `src/premap.c` (`NetLostCheck()`) | **Shared with jfSW** (jfSW's L18 left it open too). Only the local relay session being lost is detected. A host or player that vanishes in the middle of a game (machine off, tab closed, process killed) is not noticed by the others: they stand still for ever with no message. Plain UDP has the same problem. | 01 review | open: a fix needs a liveness rule in the game (jfSW's brief 05 has `NetGoneCheck()` with `mmulti_vnet_silentms()`); not done here |
| D1 | `src/game.c` `getpackets()`, packet 6 (player announcement), `:635-665` | **Game-breaking candidate (3 or more players), reproduces, needs its own brief.** In master/slave mode the master re-sends a slave's announcement to the other slaves, who file it under `other`, the machine it came from (the master), not under the index inside the packet (`packbuf[1]`; the code even says so in a `debugprintf`, "announcement with mismatched index"). So on a slave other than the sender, the name, the 10 weapon preferences (`ud.wchoice`) and `aim_mode`, `auto_aim`, `weaponswitch` of **the master** are overwritten with those of the last other slave to announce, and the other slaves' entries are never set (they stay 0: `ps[2].auto_aim` is 0 on slave 1 and 1 on the master). The simulation reads them: `auto_aim` of the *target* player decides whether a shot is aimed at it (`player.c:224`), `weaponswitch` the weapon a pickup selects (`actors.c:206`, `gamedef.c:2558`), `wchoice` the weapon chosen when one runs out of ammo. So with three or more players the machines can disagree about a shot or a pickup. (Default settings hide most of it; the names are wrong on slaves too.) Two players are not affected. | 01: the first 3-player `-testdump` (`players` hash) differed at tic 1 | open. The fix is to use `packbuf[1]` (checked against `other` only when the master sent it) in `case 6`. `-testdump` leaves these fields out until it is fixed (`src/testhooks.c`). |
| D2 | `src/game.c` `spawn()`, `:4671-4676` | The camera effectors (SE 27) are made visible (`xrepeat = yrepeat = 64`, `cstat &= 32767`) only when `ud.recstat == 1` at spawn, i.e. in the game that records a demo; the game that plays the demo back spawns them with the map's values (recstat is set to 2 after `newgame`). So a recording game and its replay differ in these sprites from tic 1. Cosmetic (camera markers drawn in demos), but it means a recording's `-testdump` never equals its playback's; two playbacks do. | 01: `test-single.sh` | open, not planned |
| D3 | `src/game.c:6714` (F4), `src/menues.c` (`menus()`) | **F4 in a game stops it in a menu that is not drawn and that Escape does not close.** F4 calls `cmenu(701)` and `menus()` has no `case 701` (the sound menu is `case 700`, reached from Options). Reproduced in the browser build (Chromium): after F4 the player cannot move and the picture is the plain level; several Escape presses change nothing. **Native too** (checked at the merge with brief 01: the native `duke3d` under Xvfb with `xdotool`, GRP and a config made by `run-headless.sh`, `-nosetup -v1 -l1 -s1`: holding Up moved the view by 33,497 pixels before F4; after F4 holding Up changed 85 pixels, and after two Escape presses 78, with the level's picture and no menu on screen). Not a browser problem and not fixed here. | 02 | **open** |
| D4 | `src/menues.c:684-760` (`probe_()`, `probekeys()`) | A menu hotkey (the main menu's N, O, L, H, C, Q and the others) moves the cursor to its item but does not choose it: `probey = k` and the function returns "no choice yet", so Enter is still needed. Whether the original 1.3d chose the item at once is not known here (not checked), so this may be intended; written down because it surprises. | 02 | **open** (unconfirmed) |

Candidates already seen while reading the code in brief 00 (written up in [`code-facts.md`](code-facts.md) §12, not yet numbered because nothing has reproduced them): `-nosetup` not stopping the startup window on a first run; a quit during the network wait returning from `Startup()` with initialisation unfinished; SIGTERM acting as the Escape key; packet 8 (user-map game) reading `ffire` from the map name and dropping its last character; `newgame` and the episode-3 end waiting for sounds to finish; the shareware order screens after a network exit; `myname` not re-sent; peer-to-peer limited to 2 players.
